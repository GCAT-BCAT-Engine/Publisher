## Proposed Volume I insert: “Further research: sovereign AI economics”

> Volume III extends the governed contribution and settlement model by examining the **net economic outcome for each individual**. It distinguishes provider savings from costs shifted to consumer devices or other infrastructure; measures the complete cost of a matched authorized and independently verifiable AI outcome; and separates hypothetical personal-data valuations from compensation actually received through authorized use. The Volume III framework is a research proposal, not proof of realized savings, data ownership in every jurisdiction or deployed payment capability.

## Proposed Volume II insert: “Economic participation and incidence”

> Persistent Human and AI Entity participation raises not only questions of agency, labor and legal standing but also of **who finances computation and who captures value from authorized personal data use**. Volume III proposes distribution-sensitive cost and compensation accounting across centralized, device-side and prospective orbital infrastructure. Economic rights and legal standing remain jurisdiction-dependent; no compensation, employment status or ownership right follows automatically from persistent Entity identity.
